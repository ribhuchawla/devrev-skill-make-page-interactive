Where should the version picker live, and how should it look?

The picker shows the baked-in commit history (title · timestamp · author) and lets you restore or branch. Three placements/styles below — pick the one that feels right, or mix.

A

Toolbar dropdown — "v9 ▾"

A compact version chip sits in the existing Annotate/View bar. Click it → a dropdown timeline drops down. Lightest footprint; reuses the bar you already have.
